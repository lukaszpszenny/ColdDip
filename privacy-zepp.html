Cold Dip for Amazfit (Zepp OS) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.
Developer: Łukasz Pszenny. Contact: lukasz.pszenny@gmail.com.

Summary

Cold Dip has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The author receives none of your data. The only data that leaves your watch and phone is an approximate position sent to a weather service, and a random account token plus the device model sent to the payment provider (see below).

Health and location data

With your permission the app reads your heart rate and, for open-water sessions, your GPS position, and stores the session (duration, water temperature, heart rate, distance, a simple route) in the app’s history on the watch. This data stays on your watch. It is not sent to the author, not used for advertising, and not sold. You can revoke heart-rate and location access in the Zepp app or watch settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the local history.

Weather (MET Norway)

When your phone is connected, the app can request the local weather (air temperature, wind, conditions). For that it sends your approximate position, with coordinates shortened to 4 decimal places (about 11 m), to MET Norway’s Locationforecast 2.0 service at api.met.no, together with an identifying application name. The service is used under the CC BY 4.0 licence. MET Norway operates it under its own terms and privacy policy. Requests are cached so they are not repeated more often than every 15 minutes. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent to the author. Attribution: © MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).

Purchases (KiezelPay)

The app is a one-time purchase (USD 4.99) with a 7-day free trial. Payments are handled by KiezelPay (api.kiezelpay.com and kzl.io). To check the licence and start a purchase the app sends a random account token and the watch’s device model; it does not send your name, e-mail, location or health data, and it never receives your payment card details. KiezelPay operates under its own terms and privacy policy. The 7-day trial clock is stored on your watch.

Data stored on the watch

Session history (the last 30 sessions), settings and the licence/trial record are stored in the watch’s local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes this data (a new install starts a new trial).

Device information

The app reads the device model so that KiezelPay can recognise the watch. This is not sent to the author.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone for the author’s use.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page (and, when the page is public, at https://lukaszpszenny.github.io/ColdDip/privacy-zepp.html).
